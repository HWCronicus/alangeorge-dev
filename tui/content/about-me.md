# About Me

Hi — I'm Alan George, a software developer who builds fun, elegant, and enjoyable user-focused web applications with a slight lean to the backend side of development. I grew up in Cocoa Beach, Florida, at a young age I started developing for the early web, learning JavaScript, HTML, PHP, Perl, and ActionScript out of curiosity and friendly competition.

After high school I joined the U.S. Army, spent several years in training to become a Critical Care Flight Paramedic. After serving for 14 years I was in the process of medical retirement. During this transition period I completed a VA-sponsored Software Engineering program and transitioned into a full-time career as a Full Stack Developer.

I currently work on production web applications and enjoy hobby projects in 3D printing, 3D printer software and hardware, IoT and smart-home devices, and home lab infrastructure. Outside of development I surf, snowboard and maintain a reef aquarium.

## Interests

- Reef Aquarium Keeping
- Application Development
- IoT/Smart Home Devices
- Home Networking
- Open source development

## Philosophy

I believe in creating FUN, elegant, efficient, and user-friendly software that makes a difference.
